# Teach-Back Log

One entry per teaching session. Round 1 is five sessions — the builder teaches their section to
all four teammates, then **each listener explains one piece back without notes**.

A session is not finished until every listener has explained something back. That is the part
that shows learning; the walkthrough on its own does not.
